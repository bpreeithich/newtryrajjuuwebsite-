Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

Choosing natural products should feel simple. Here are a few things you might want to know before placing your order.

What is Raajjuu's Native and Naturals?

Raajjuu's Native and Naturals is a Kotagiri-based store focused on bringing natural products directly from farmers to customers. We source, carefully pack, and deliver products to your doorstep.

Where do you source your products from?

Our products come directly from farmers in and around the Nilgiris.

Are your products 100% natural?

We focus on natural, minimally processed products. Ingredient details are shared with each product.

Do you provide home delivery?

Yes, we deliver products to your doorstep.

How can I place an order?

Fill in the enquiry form above and our team will get in touch with you.

Do you deliver outside Kotagiri?

Please contact us with your location and we will let you know.

How can I know more about a particular product?

Use the enquiry form or call us, and we will share the details you need.
